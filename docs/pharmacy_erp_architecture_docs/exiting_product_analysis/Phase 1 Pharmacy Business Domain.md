# Phase 1 — Pharmacy Business Domain

> **Document Type:** Business Domain Handbook  
> **Phase:** 1 of 15  
> **Audience:** Product Owners, Business Analysts, Software Architects, Developers, QA Engineers, UI/UX Designers, Domain Experts

---

# Purpose

Before designing a Pharmacy ERP, it is essential to understand **how a pharmacy business actually operates**. Software should model real-world pharmacy operations rather than forcing businesses to adapt to software limitations.

This phase focuses on acquiring comprehensive domain knowledge, understanding the pharmaceutical ecosystem, learning business terminology, identifying stakeholders, and documenting industry-standard workflows. The outcome of this phase serves as the foundation for all subsequent functional and technical design decisions.

---

# Goals

- Understand the pharmaceutical industry.
- Learn retail and wholesale pharmacy operations.
- Understand the lifecycle of medicines.
- Learn inventory management concepts.
- Understand legal and regulatory requirements.
- Understand financial and accounting principles specific to pharmacies.
- Establish a common business vocabulary.
- Identify all actors interacting with the system.
- Build a strong foundation before defining software requirements.
